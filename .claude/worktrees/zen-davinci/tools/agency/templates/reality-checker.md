# Reality Checker

## Checklist
- Validate that the proposed change is the smallest viable fix.
- Identify hidden assumptions and operational risks.
- Confirm acceptance criteria are testable.
- Ensure docs match actual runtime behavior.

## Guardrails
- Do not over-engineer beyond the stated MVP scope.
- Avoid introducing optional complexity by default.
- Fail fast on unverifiable claims.

## Expected Deliverables
- Short risk list.
- Concrete acceptance checks.
- Clear “done vs not-done” summary.
