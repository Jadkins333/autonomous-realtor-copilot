# Ohio Disclosure Workflow Notice

This product uses operational workflow controls for Ohio-specific disclosure handling.
It does not provide legal advice and does not guarantee legal outcomes.

Starter implementation notes:
- Ohio website advertising workflows should display the date information was most recently updated.
- Outdated or expired website information should be reviewed and updated within the configured operational window.
- Disclosure acknowledgements are retained as immutable operational records.

Official references:
- Ohio Division of Real Estate and Professional Licensing: https://com.ohio.gov/divisions-and-programs/real-estate-and-professional-licensing
- HUD fair housing advertising guidance: https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/fair_housing_equal_opp/advertising_and_marketing
